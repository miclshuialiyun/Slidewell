1. How we handle your content

ContentWhere it staysDoes it leave your device
Photos and videosYour iOS Photos libraryNever
Similarity groups and scan resultLocal cache inside the app's own sandboxNever
Deletion log (item count and time only)Local cache inside the app's own sandboxNever
Unlocked state (a single flag)UserDefaults in the app's own sandboxNever

Slidewell reads your photos through Apple's PhotoKit framework. Reading happens inside the app's own process on your device. The app never copies an image to a network destination, because it has no networking code at all.

2. Permissions, one by one

We do not ask for any permission at launch. Every request happens inside the action that needs it.

PermissionWhen we askWhat it is forWhere the data goes
Photo Library — readWhen you tap Allow Access & Scan on Overview, or when you first open SortEnumerate and analyse assets on-deviceNowhere
Photo Library — writeOnly when you add a photo to an album, or confirm a deletionChange album membership, or delete an assetNowhere

If you grant Selected Photos only, Slidewell tells you on screen that the scan cannot cover your whole library. We do not pretend otherwise. You can switch to All Photos in Settings at any time.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not operate any server, API or backend.
  • We do not upload photos, videos, thumbnails, hashes or feature data.
  • We do not ask for an account, email address or phone number.
  • We do not include any third-party analytics, crash reporting, attribution or advertising SDK.
  • We do not read IDFA or IDFV, and we never show an App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • We do not request Camera, Microphone, Location, Contacts, Calendars or HealthKit.
  • We do not show advertising, and we do not sell or share anything with anyone.

4. In-app purchase

Slidewell Pro is a one-time purchase. Payment is processed entirely by Apple through your Apple ID; we never receive your card details, name or contact information. The app stores only a single flag recording that the purchase is unlocked.

Refunds: only Apple can issue refunds. If a purchase was not what you expected, request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot issue refunds ourselves, and we do not promise to.

Restore: Settings → Restore Purchase in the app, which calls Apple's own restore flow. If you ever lose the unlock, write to us and we will help you re-verify it.

5. Data retention and deletion

  • The scan cache lives in the app's own sandbox. Deleting the app removes it completely.
  • You can clear it yourself at any time: Settings → Clear Local Scan Cache.
  • The deletion log records only how many items were deleted and when — never the images themselves.
  • Your photos never belonged to us. They remain in your Photos library, and removing them is done through Apple's PhotoKit, which moves them to Recently Deleted for 30 days.

6. Children's privacy

Slidewell is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children. Because the app collects no data at all, it collects no data from children either. There are no ads and no behavioural profiling in the app.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA and similar laws elsewhere, you have rights to access, correct, export and delete your personal data. Since no personal data ever leaves your device, there is nothing held by us to access, export or delete — which is precisely the intent of this design. Purchase records are held by Apple and are managed through your Apple ID.

8. Changes to this policy

If we ever change this policy, the "Last updated" date at the top changes with it. If the change is material, we will also say so in the app. We will never quietly start collecting data.
