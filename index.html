Swipe through your photo library one frame at a time, or scan it in bulk for duplicates, screenshots and space-hogging videos. Everything runs on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded.

Slidewell is a one-time purchase. No subscription, no auto-renewal, no ads — and once you unlock it, every future update is included. We do not charge twice.

What it does

Sort with a swipe

One photo at a time. Drag a card left, right, up or down — the card labels each direction while you drag, so you never guess.

File into albums

Swipe up to drop a photo into any album, or create a new one right there without leaving the app.

Undo anything

Every swipe can be taken back. Deletions land in an in-app trash first, so Undo genuinely works.

Find duplicates

Perceptual hashing narrows the field, Vision feature prints confirm it. Groups come sorted by how much space they free.

Clear screenshots and videos

Screenshots get their own category; videos over 80 MB surface ranked by size.

Review before deleting

A confirmation list, then a second confirmation. Nothing disappears by surprise.

What it does not do

We would rather lose you here than disappoint you after you install it.

  • No cloud upload, account or sign-in — there is no Slidewell server, at all.
  • No ads of any kind, ever. This is not a promise about a free tier; there is no ad tier.
  • No subscription. Charging every year to delete junk is a bad deal, so we don't.
  • No background or automatic deleting. The app waits for your finger every single time.
  • No iPad version. Slidewell is built for iPhone only.
  • No network access whatsoever. The app opens zero connections.
