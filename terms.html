1. The service

Slidewell is an iPhone app that helps you sort and remove photos and videos already stored on your device. It provides:

  • a swipe-based sorting flow that files photos into albums, marks favourites, or moves them to trash;
  • a scan that groups similar photos, collects screenshots, and lists videos over 80 MB;
  • a review step before anything is deleted.

Slidewell is not a backup service, a cloud storage service, or a photo recovery tool. It deletes things; it does not keep a copy of them.

Deleting is permanent. Deleted items move to your Photos app's Recently Deleted album for 30 days, then are erased. With iCloud Photos enabled, a deletion here also deletes the item on every device signed in to the same Apple ID. You are responsible for what you choose to delete.
