Frequently asked questions

Is Slidewell free?

Yes, with limits. You can sort up to 200 photos a day, scan your whole library, and delete up to 10 items per batch — free, and with no ads. Slidewell Pro removes the limits for one payment.

Is there a subscription?

No. There never has been and there won't be one. Slidewell Pro is a one-time purchase of USD 19.99. Nothing renews, so there is nothing to cancel.

Are there ads?

None, in any part of the app, free or paid. We do not ship an ad SDK, and we do not plan to introduce one.

Do my photos leave my iPhone?

No. The app has no networking code, so it cannot upload anything. Similarity analysis uses Apple's own on-device Vision framework. See the privacy policy for the full table.

Why does it ask for photo permission?

Only when you tap Allow Access & Scan, or first open Sort. We never ask at launch. Without the permission there is nothing for the app to look at.

I only granted Selected Photos. What happens?

The scan covers only what you selected, and the app says so on screen rather than pretending it scanned everything. Switch to All Photos in Settings for a complete scan.

I deleted something by accident. Can I get it back?

Yes, for 30 days. Open your Photos app → Albums → Recently Deleted, and recover it. After 30 days it is gone. Inside Slidewell, deletions sit in the in-app trash until you confirm, and you can undo a swipe at any time before that.

Will deleting here delete photos on my other devices?

If iCloud Photos is on, yes — deleting removes the item everywhere signed in to the same Apple ID. The app warns you about this on the confirmation screen.

Why didn't it find some duplicates?

Similarity detection compares perceptual hashes and Vision feature prints. Heavily edited, cropped, or recoloured versions of the same shot can fall outside the threshold. Suggestions are a starting point, not a verdict.

It didn't find any similar photos at all.

Either your library genuinely has few near-duplicates, or access is limited to Selected Photos. Check the permission status in Settings.

I have 20,000+ photos. Will it cope?

Yes. The app holds identifiers, not images, and loads each photo only while it is the top card. The first full scan of a very large library can take a few minutes; you can cancel it at any time and results are cached.

How do I restore my purchase?

Settings → Restore Purchase. It calls Apple's own restore flow, so it works after a reinstall or on a new phone, as long as you use the same Apple ID. If it still says not unlocked, contact us.

How do I get a refund?

Only Apple can issue refunds. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and request one there. We cannot process refunds directly, but we will help you through it.

Does it work on iPad or Mac?

No. Slidewell is iPhone only, and requires iOS 17.0 or later.

Which languages does it support?

English and Simplified Chinese. The app follows your system language — there is no in-app switch. Other languages fall back to English.

How do I change the swipe directions?

Settings → Swap swipe directions. By default dragging right keeps a photo and left trashes it; the toggle flips them.

Your data

Slidewell has no server and collects nothing. The full breakdown is in the privacy policy.
